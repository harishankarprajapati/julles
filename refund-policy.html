Refund Policy

At Julles, we are committed to providing high-quality Pilates sessions and wellness services. Please read our refund policy carefully before purchasing any membership, package, or class.

1. Membership & Package Payments

All membership fees and class package payments made at Julles are non-refundable and non-transferable. Once a membership or package has been activated, no refunds will be issued.

2. Class Cancellations

Clients must cancel or reschedule a booked class at least 12 hours in advance. Failure to do so may result in the session being marked as used, with no refund or replacement.

3. Missed Sessions

Any missed sessions due to late arrival, no-shows, or personal reasons will not be refunded or rescheduled.

4. Studio Closures

In the rare event that Julles cancels a class due to unforeseen circumstances, the session will be rescheduled or credited to your account. No cash refunds will be provided.

5. Medical Exceptions

Refunds or extensions may be considered only in exceptional medical cases, subject to valid medical documentation and management approval.

6. Promotional Offers

Promotional, discounted, or trial packages are strictly non-refundable and cannot be exchanged or extended.

7. Payment Disputes

Any payment disputes must be reported within 7 days of the transaction date. Disputes raised after this period may not be considered.

8. Policy Updates

Julles reserves the right to modify or update this Refund Policy at any time without prior notice. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting on the website.

9. Contact Us

For any questions regarding this Refund Policy, please contact us directly at our studio or through our official communication channels.
